Resonance 1.0.1

Fixes for Bluetooth audio, the permission prompt, overlays and recording

Fixed

  • System audio now plays at the right speed with Bluetooth headphones and other devices that aren't at 48 kHz (AirPods run at 24 kHz): the beat, tempo and waveform no longer run at half speed, and listening restarts when you switch output devices.
  • Resonance no longer freezes while macOS is asking for the System Audio Recording permission.
  • The live wallpaper (behind windows) now appears above the desktop picture and any other live wallpaper.
  • The on-top overlay's screen blend now shows: dark areas are see-through and light glows over your windows.
  • Recorded clips: full-length audio (it was cut short), a steady 60 fps, and recording can start right as fullscreen opens.
  • If music is playing but Resonance hears only silence, it now explains how to allow System Audio Recording.
  • The mini player's visuals fill the whole window.
